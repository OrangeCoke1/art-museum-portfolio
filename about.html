Gallery Walk is an interactive digital museum website designed around the idea of

"Slowing down the act of looking"

On the web, artworks are often consumed quickly as images, with little time spent on their details, context, or material qualities. This project aims to create a more patient viewing experience by allowing users to engage with artworks through scrolling, clicking, dragging, zooming, and rotating.

Each art form is presented through a different interaction model. Paintings are displayed in a horizontal gallery with classical frames, where the centered artwork gradually becomes clearer as if a museum light has been turned on. Sculptures are shown with pedestals and 3D model previews, allowing users to explore their volume, material, and form from different angles. Photography is presented through the visual language of photographic prints and darkroom development, emphasizing tone, contrast, and the process of revealing an image.

The website also provides detailed artwork information, including artist, year, medium, dimensions, location, subject, and collection data. By combining visual interaction with contextual information, the project encourages users to move beyond passive browsing and spend more time understanding each work.

The goal of Gallery Walk is to transform digital art viewing into a slower, more immersive, and more meaningful experience.
